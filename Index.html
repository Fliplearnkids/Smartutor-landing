About StudySmart NG

StudySmart NG is a Nigerian edtech platform founded to solve one big problem: Many JSS and SSS students struggle with exams because they lack access to quality, affordable learning materials.

We provide structured online tutoring, past questions, and study notes that make learning simple. Our mission is to help every student, no matter their background, pass WAEC, NECO, and JAMB with confidence.

Located in Owode Ede, we are building the future of education in Nigeria, one student at a time.

Our Vision

To be the #1 exam preparation platform for secondary school students in Nigeria.

Our Mission

To make quality education accessible, affordable, and effective for every Nigerian student.

Our Commitment: SDG 4 - Quality Education

StudySmart NG directly supports United Nations Sustainable Development Goal 4: "Ensure inclusive and equitable quality education and promote lifelong learning opportunities for all."

How we contribute:

1. Access: We take quality tutoring to students in rural and urban areas who cannot afford private lessons.

2. Equity: Our affordable fees ensure that poor students are not left behind.

3. Quality: We use proven teaching methods and updated curriculum to ensure students actually learn and pass.

By 2030, we aim to help 100,000 Nigerian students achieve their academic goals.
